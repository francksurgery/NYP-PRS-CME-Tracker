Adding to your PowerPoint

Download both PNGs above, then follow these steps to add them to your intro slide:

1
Open your PowerPoint and navigate to the first slide.
2
Go to Insert → Pictures → Picture from File and select each downloaded PNG.
3
Place the CME Tracker QR on the left (for physicians) and the Attendance QR on the right (for everyone).
4
Add a brief label under each: "Scan to track CME" and "Scan to log attendance."
Reminder: These QR codes only work once your files are live on GitHub Pages. If you haven't done that step yet, complete the GitHub setup first, then come back here to generate the final codes.
